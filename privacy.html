Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

VYLOOP is a content management and automation project designed to help users organize video production, social media workflows, creator campaigns, and performance analytics.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when using VYLOOP and its optional integrations.

1. Information We Process

Depending on the features enabled, VYLOOP may process:

  • Social media account identifiers and profile information.
  • Video identifiers and published video URLs.
  • Video performance metrics, including views, likes, comments and shares.
  • Campaign information and financial records entered by users.
  • Content uploaded or created within the application.
  • Authorization credentials for integrations explicitly connected by users.

2. TikTok Integration

VYLOOP is developing an optional integration with TikTok using official TikTok APIs.

When available and explicitly authorized, this integration may retrieve information about the user's own TikTok account and published videos.

Requested permissions may include user.info.basic and video.list.

VYLOOP will request access only through TikTok's official authorization process.

The integration is currently under development. The public VYLOOP website does not independently collect TikTok account data.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is processed to provide application functionality, including content organization, campaign tracking, performance analytics, and financial reporting.

Authorized TikTok information, when integration becomes available, will be used to display video statistics and support the user's content management workflows.

4. Data Storage and Security

VYLOOP is currently designed primarily as a locally operated application.

Application information is stored in the user's configured environment.

The application includes security controls such as authentication and encrypted credential storage.

No system can guarantee absolute security. Users are responsible for securing their own devices, accounts, and application environments.

5. Third-Party Services

Optional features may interact with third-party services, including social media platforms, AI generation providers, and campaign partners.

Information sent to third-party services is also subject to their respective privacy policies.

Users should review the terms and privacy practices of each connected service.

6. Data Sharing

VYLOOP does not operate a business that sells users' personal information.

Information may be transmitted to third-party providers when users enable integrations or explicitly initiate related operations.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored application data remains in the configured environment until it is deleted or otherwise managed by the application owner.

Users may disconnect authorized integrations through supported application controls or through the relevant third-party platform.

Revoking access may prevent future API requests but does not necessarily delete information previously stored in the application.

8. Public Website

The public VYLOOP website is informational and is hosted using GitHub Pages.

The website does not intentionally implement advertising trackers or behavioral analytics. GitHub may process technical information associated with hosting and delivering the website.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as VYLOOP develops new features or integrations.

The latest version will be available on this website.
